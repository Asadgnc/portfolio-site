About Me 🙋‍♂️

My story, my passion, and the logic that drives my code.

My Story

I’ve been fascinated by the mysteries of the universe since I was a kid. While most of my friends were playing games, I was reading about stars, black holes, and quantum mechanics, constantly asking why and how. That curiosity led me into physics, where I discovered a world of patterns, logic, and endless questions. Later, I realized that artificial intelligence shares that same wonder — uncovering hidden structures, solving complex problems, and learning from data.

Finding My Path

During my academic journey, I explored many avenues: from research in physics and AI projects to mentoring students in exam prep and academic growth. I’ve helped learners prepare for the SAT, TOEFL, and IELTS, reviewed essays and CVs, and guided them in planning their studies and career paths. Mentoring taught me that knowledge is most powerful when it’s shared — when people gain both confidence and understanding.

Pursuing My Passion

I combine my love for science and technology with a drive to make learning fun and meaningful. I enjoy breaking down difficult concepts into intuitive insights, building learning strategies, and supporting students to achieve real results. My work isn’t just about grades or scores; it’s about curiosity, growth, and empowerment.

Looking Ahead

Every project, research paper, and mentoring session is part of a bigger goal: creating a bridge between science, technology, and people. I want to inspire curiosity, foster understanding, and help others realize their potential — because learning is a journey we take together, and I’m excited to share that journey with anyone willing to explore.
