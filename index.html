About Me

Hey, I'm Mert Hakan! 👋 My world revolves around turning big, messy ideas into clean, functional code. I'm not just a Computer Engineering candidate; I'm a passionate builder driven by boundless curiosity and a desire to make digital things *work*. You could say I’m fluent in the language of logic, but I also speak fluent creativity. 🧠✨

The projects you see here—from the web apps to the early dives into Blockchain—are a result of sleepless nights and pure excitement. They represent my journey from high school valedictorian 🥇 to a developer eager to tackle the next big challenge. I believe the best solutions are born when analytical thinking meets a flair for design (that's why this site has cool particle effects! 😉).

My core mission? To dive deep into the worlds of AI and distributed systems, pushing boundaries and proving that complexity can be beautiful. I'm here to build, learn, and connect. If you're looking for someone who brings both meticulous detail and genuine enthusiasm to the table, let's chat! 🚀
